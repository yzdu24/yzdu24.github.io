I am currently a Ph.D. student in the field of molecular electronics at the Max Planck Institute for Polymer Research (MPI-P), in the department headed by Prof. Paul Blom. I am supervised by Dr. Denis Andrienko and Dr. Gert-Jan Wetzelaer.

Before joining MPI-P, I received my M.Sc. in Physics from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, where I worked under the supervision of Prof. Yi Wang. I also participated in a study-abroad program in Electrical Engineering at Khalifa University under the supervision of Dr. Ammar Nayfeh. I received my B.Eng. in Energy Storage Science and Engineering from China University of Petroleum (Beijing).

My current research focuses on molecular dynamics simulations of materials for OLED emissive layers and the fabrication of blue OLEDs with long operational lifetimes.

### Research Interests

- Machine learning for materials
- Molecular dynamics simulations(e.g. coacervates, proteins)
- Quantum computing(e.g. Ising machines)
- Perovskite solar cells
- Redox flow batteries
- Lithium-ion batteries
- Electrocatalysis
